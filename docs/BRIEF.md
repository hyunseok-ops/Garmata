# Garage Intelligence 360: briefing for an outside reviewer

This briefing is for someone (human or AI) who hasn't seen the project and should be able to critique it and propose
better approaches. The repository is public: https://github.com/hyunseok-ops/Garmata (branch `main`). Every file path below is
relative to that repo, e.g. https://github.com/hyunseok-ops/Garmata/blob/main/pipeline/spin_build.py.

## 1. The product goal
Garage is a marketplace for used fire trucks and other municipal vehicles. Each listing has ~50–200 seller photos, but only a
handful are full exterior shots, and their labels are unreliable. We want an interactive **360° spin** of the truck on the listing
page.

Requirements, in the owner's words and priority order:
1. "The image itself is revolving 360 with the images we have": a photo turntable, not a 3D model.
2. "Include the actual background": the real parking lot, not a cut-out on white.
3. "Really smooth": scrolling and dragging must feel continuous, never laggy.
4. "No modification on the truck": real photos are shown as they are (crop/scale only).
5. "If we are lacking views AI can generate it": generated angles are fine where no photo exists.

Quality "doesn't have to be crazy", but it must not look broken while someone slowly scrolls.

## 2. What exists today (pilot: 2009 Pierce Velocity Pumper, listing #226894)
The best result is spin **v15**: 192 frames.
- **Real photos:** 8 of the key frames are real listing photos, shown unmodified.
- **AI angles:** 40 key frames are generated by Stable Virtual Camera (SEVA).
- **In-betweens:** 144 are optical-flow frames from RIFE, 3 between each key frame.

The desktop app (Electron + React) shows it with drag, momentum, trackpad scroll, preset buttons, a "Real listing photo" /
"AI-generated angle" badge, and projected part tags. It runs at 120 fps.

An earlier approach, a Gaussian splat trained on AI-generated views, looked blurry and whitish. It is kept but is not the product path.

## 3. Pipeline, step by step

### 3.1 Coordinate convention and the ring
- **Axes:** vehicle at the origin, front faces +X, driver side +Z. OpenGL cameras (look down −Z, +Y up).
- **Cameras:** on a horizontal ring around the truck at 7.5° steps, 48 cameras. `azimuth 0` = in front, 90 = driver side,
  180 = rear, 270 = officer (passenger) side.
- **Ring:** `{radius 2.5, height 0.35, target [0, 0.35, 0], 1024×768, hfov 60°}` in normalised units.
- **Code:** `src/data/novel.ts` has `poseFor`, `ringFor`, `planRing`, `buildTransforms`; tests are in `src/data/novel.test.ts`.

### 3.2 Choosing and placing real photos
- **Labels don't give angles.** Listing photos carry a coarse label (`front`, `front_34`, `side`, `rear_34`, `rear`, plus interior and
  detail labels) and no left/right. The labels are often wrong: front-3/4 shots labelled `front`, rear-3/4 labelled `side`.
- **Photos are pinned to angles** in `experiments/novel-view/<listingId>/azimuths.json` (not committed; example below).
  1. **Facing detection** (`pipeline/facing.py`) is a conservative SIFT + RANSAC guess at which side each 3/4 or side photo shows.
  2. **Registration against an earlier generated lap** (`pipeline/spin_build.py`, `registrations.json`) matches truck-only SIFT
     features (rembg matte as the mask) against each lap frame, and keeps a similarity transform with scale and inlier checks.
     Photos of a side the lap never saw are matched **mirrored** against the opposite side, giving angle 360 − a.
  3. **Manual pins by eye** where both fail. One photo was mis-sided by me (placed at 30° on the driver side; it was an
     officer-side shot at ~345°). That broke 22–45° until fixed.
- **Pilot pins (16 inputs):** 0, 30, 37.5, 82.5, 90, 135, 180, 217.5, 225, 232.5, 270, 307.5, 315, 322.5, 330, 345.
  The driver side has no photos between 37.5° and 82.5°.

```json
{ "photos": { "<imageId>": 90, "...": 135 }, "labels": { "front_34": 322.5 },
  "regenerateReals": true, "ringStepDeg": 7.5, "keepBackground": true, "ringSize": [1024, 768],
  "seed": 7, "spinReplace": [60] }
```

### 3.3 Generating the lap (SEVA on Modal)
- **Model:** Stable Virtual Camera (stabilityai/stable-virtual-camera, non-commercial license). `img2img` task, reconfusion data
  format (transforms.json + train_test_split). It runs on a Modal H100 in about 20 min for 16 inputs → 48 targets. Code:
  `pipeline/novel_view_modal.py`.
- **Real photos are inputs only (`regenerateReals`).** All 48 ring angles, the real ones included, are generated, so the lap is
  self-consistent. The real photos come back in step 3.4.
- **Pose normalisation (`keepBackground`).** Photos are shot at different distances. Instead of shrinking or padding them, which
  made SEVA copy picture-frame borders, each input camera is moved along its view ray by `k`. `k` = projected vehicle-box height
  divided by the rembg truck height in the photo.
- **16 inputs need a different SEVA mode.** With ≥ 9 inputs SEVA's default chunking crashes (`T` becomes a list), so we use its
  documented orbit mode: `--use_traj_prior True --traj_prior orbit --chunk_strategy nearest-gt`.
- **Resolution** is 1024×768. It was 768×576 before; 1024 is sharper and SEVA handled it.
- **Orchestration:** `electron/novel.ts` does prep, spawn, poll, download and a per-generation lock. The Modal CLI shim is
  `pipeline/gi.py`. Headless entry points are in `electron/main.ts` (`GI_NOVEL_PREP`, `GI_NOVEL_GENERATE`, `GI_SPIN_BUILD`, …).

### 3.4 Assembling the spin (`pipeline/spin_build.py`)
1. **Register** each exterior photo to its best lap slot. Truck-matte SIFT, greedy assignment with one photo per slot, and the
   label window widened ±30°.
2. **Real frames are plain crops of the photo, never composited.** `real_view()` computes the crop that shows what the ring
   camera shows. If the photo can't cover the frame, the neighbouring generated frames zoom in instead of the photo being shrunk.
   The cap is 1.6× zoom; photos needing more are skipped, and close-ups fail here.
3. **Generated frames get a zoom and centre** interpolated between the neighbouring real frames, so the size doesn't jump.
4. **Size check** on the finished frames: a real frame whose truck height (rembg) is > 12% off its neighbours is dropped. It
   dropped 3 photos in v15.
5. **`spinReplace`** substitutes reviewed-bad generated frames with the RIFE midpoint of their neighbours (60° in v15).
6. **RIFE 4×** (rife-ncnn-vulkan v4.6, local GPU) adds in-betweens around the closed loop. Key frames are copied through
   untouched.
7. **Output:** `spin.json` = `{w, h, ring, frames: [{azimuth, file, source: real|generated|interpolated, sourceImageId, view: [zoom, cx, cy], key}]}`.

### 3.5 Viewer (`src/viewer/SpinViewer.tsx`)
- **Rendering:** a canvas with contain-fit, cross-fading the two frames around the current angle.
- **Input:** drag with momentum, trackpad/wheel, arrow keys, and preset easing.
- **Memory:** key frames stay decoded as ImageBitmaps; in-betweens are decoded off-thread in a ±16 window around the current
  frame, about 70 of 192 in memory. If a spin outruns the window, it blends the surrounding key frames.
- **Tags** are projected from each frame's ring camera plus its zoom and crop. Today they come from a generic pumper template.
- **Smoke test** (`electron/smoke.ts`, `GI_SMOKE_SPIN=1`, `GI_SMOKE_SPIN_WALK=3.75`) takes screenshots and fps samples, including a
  human-paced slow scroll.

## 4. History: what was tried and why it changed
| Attempt | Result | Lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meshy image-to-3D meshes | Plausible but invented geometry | Removed; not faithful to the real truck |
| SEVA views on white + Gaussian splat (v5–v10) | Blurry, whitish, floaters, needle "shards" from above | A splat averages inconsistent AI views → blur. Scale regularisation fixed shards |
| Photo spin, real photos pasted over AI frames | Visible rectangles; size jumps | Never composite; crop only |
| Alignment on all features | Wrong scale (background dominates) | Align on the truck matte only |
| 7 inputs at 768×576 (v13) | Driver side invented (wrong cab/grille), rear melted | Need inputs on every side |
| 14–16 inputs at 1024×768 (v14–v15) | Much sharper; correct rear and sides | Remaining problems are where no photo exists |
| Remove a close-up input to kill a smear | Smear gone, ghost wheels appeared | Defects move around; seeds and inputs trade artifacts |

## 5. Weak points in v15, from a slow manual scroll (screenshot every 3.75°)
**Truck in AI angles**
- **Driver-side front, 0–60°: weakest.** There are no photos between 37.5° and 82.5°. The far side of the body ghosts
  (doubled outline) at 4–26°. The cab is smeared at 41° and a blur at 56–64°. 60° was AI-broken, with doubled wheels, and is
  now a flow blend.
- **Rear, 184–206°:** a brown smear band in the background; the truck looks stretched; doubled rear wheels at 206°.
- **Small artefacts:** ghost wheels and smudges under the body at 150–165° and 236–247°; smeared treetops at the top of the frame
  in several places.

**Framing**
- **Zoom "breathing" at 300–341°:** real frames with different framing alternate with AI frames zooming to meet them.
- **Cropping:** the rear of the truck is cut off at the right edge from ~285° to 341°.
- **Jumps** at 247→251° and 266→270°, and the truck grows toward the front (0–30°).

**Tags:** template positions, not per truck. "Pump Panel" sits on the cab front; "Engine" and "Rear" float in empty space and get
clipped; labels overlap at the rear.

**App UX**
- **Stuck generating state.** A paused job shows as "Generating intermediate views" forever, and the Generate button stays disabled.
- **Review cards.** They are indistinguishable, and there are no thumbnails.
- **Layout.** The spin uses only about half the viewport height.

**Process:** each listing needs hand review of photo angles. Registering photos against an AI lap is circular: where the lap is
wrong, photos don't match it.

## 6. Open questions we'd like ideas on
1. **Real camera poses.** Would structure-from-motion (COLMAP / hloc / MASt3R / DUSt3R / VGGT) on ~15–30 exterior photos of a
   parked truck, with textured asphalt and a background, reliably give real camera poses? Feeding SEVA true poses (distance,
   height, focal) instead of an assumed ring seems like the biggest lever.
2. **Better generator.** Is there a model better suited than SEVA to a closed 360° orbit around a vehicle with a real background,
   e.g. video diffusion with camera control, or multi-view diffusion that keeps the background?
3. **Enforcing consistency.** Should we fit a 3D representation (splat / NeRF) to real photos plus AI views and render the spin
   from it? The trade-off: consistent geometry, but softer detail than the AI frames.
4. **Keeping the size consistent** without cropping or visible zoom pulses, when real photos are shot at different distances.
5. **Detecting broken AI frames automatically** (doubled wheels, smeared cab) so no one has to review them.
6. **Tags:** how to place part tags per truck automatically, e.g. a segmentation or vision model on the real photos plus poses.
7. **Capture guidance:** what minimal photo set to ask sellers for so a spin is always clean.
